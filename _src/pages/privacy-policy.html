Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Humans of English website (humansofenglish.com) collects, and how it is used. We've written it in plain English and kept it short, because the website collects very little.

The short version

  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not set cookies.
  • Our fonts, images and scripts are served from our own website, not from third-party services.
  • The contact form does not store anything on this website. It prepares an email in your own email app, and we only receive what you choose to send.

The contact form and email

The contact form checks your details in your browser, then opens your own email app with a message to mansi@humansofenglish.com already filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app, and the website itself does not store or forward anything you type.

When you email us, we receive your name, email address, learning goal and any message you include. We use these only to reply to you and to help you plan your learning. We don't share them with anyone else or add you to a mailing list. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time by emailing the same address.

Instagram

You can also message us on Instagram at @humansofenglish. Messages sent there are handled by Instagram under Instagram's privacy policy. We only use them to reply to you.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit any website, your browser shares technical information such as your IP address with the server. GitHub may log this information for security and to keep the service running. You can read GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as official exam providers. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Your rights

The website itself does not collect or store personal information. If you have emailed or messaged us, you can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent by writing to mansi@humansofenglish.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website develops. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
