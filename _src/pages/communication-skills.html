Speaking & Fluency

Fluency isn't speaking fast. It's keeping your message moving, even when you don't know a word. We practise real conversations at your level, so speaking becomes a habit instead of a test.

  • {{icon:check}}Everyday conversation: introductions, opinions, stories and questions
  • {{icon:check}}Strategies for when you get stuck: paraphrasing and buying time naturally
  • {{icon:check}}Extending answers with reasons and examples
  • {{icon:check}}Recording and reviewing yourself to track progress

{{icon:lightbulb}}Try this today: describe your morning out loud for one minute, without stopping. Then do it again and add one new detail.

Listening & Pronunciation

Listening and pronunciation are two sides of the same skill. When you know how words link and shrink in natural speech, you understand more, and you're easier to understand.

  • {{icon:check}}Word stress and sentence stress, which carry meaning in English
  • {{icon:check}}Linking and weak forms, like "gonna", "wanna" and "kind of"
  • {{icon:check}}Sounds that are tricky for speakers of your first language
  • {{icon:check}}Listening for gist, detail and the speaker's attitude

Reading & Comprehension

Good readers don't read every word the same way. We practise choosing the right strategy for the job, whether that's an email, a news story, an academic article or an exam passage.

  • {{icon:check}}Skimming for the main idea and scanning for specific details
  • {{icon:check}}Understanding the writer's opinion, purpose and tone
  • {{icon:check}}Guessing meaning from context instead of stopping for every word
  • {{icon:check}}Building reading speed with texts at the right level

{{icon:lightbulb}}Try this today: read a short news article for two minutes, then summarise it in two sentences without looking back.

Professional English

English for interviews, meetings and the workplace.

Workplace English has its own rhythm, with polite directness, clear updates and confident introductions. We focus on the situations you face at work, using your own job and industry as the material.

{{icon:users}}Interviews

Introduce yourself, describe your experience with clear examples, and ask thoughtful questions at the end.

{{icon:presentation}}Presentations

Structure a talk, signpost clearly, and explain data or ideas to people outside your field.

{{icon:messages-square}}Meetings

Give updates, agree and disagree politely, interrupt naturally and summarise next steps.

{{icon:mail}}Emails & messages

Write clear, friendly and appropriately formal messages, from quick replies to important requests.
