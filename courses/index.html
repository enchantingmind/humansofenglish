Your study plan

How your study plan comes together

Every course follows a structured plan, and we shape it around you: your level, your goal and the time you have.

Plan my course

  1. Tell us your goal

    Choose a course and share your target, your deadline and what feels hardest right now.

  2. Find your starting point

    A short check of your current English shows where to focus first.

  3. Get your study plan

    A week-by-week plan with clear milestones, adjusted to your pace.

  4. Learn and practise

    Live sessions in person or online, with regular practice between them.

  5. Review your progress

    Personal feedback and check-ins so you can see how far you've come.

Two ways to learn

In person or online, your choice

Every course is available in both formats, with the same study plan, the same materials and the same personal feedback.

In person

Face-to-face sessions for learners who like to practise in the room, with real-time conversation and feedback.

Online, over a video call

Live, interactive sessions from home or work. All you need is a stable internet connection and a device with a microphone.
