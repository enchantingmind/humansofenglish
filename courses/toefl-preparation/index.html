Course

TOEFL Preparation

Academic English, note-taking and timed practice for every section of the TOEFL iBT.

  • 8–12 weeks
  • Intermediate and above

Course fees

From ₹999/ month

  • In person
  • Online (video call)
Enquire about this course

Your exact fee depends on the plan you choose, and we confirm it with you before you start.

Is it for you?

Who this course is for

  • Students applying to universities that accept TOEFL
  • Learners who want to build strong academic English
  • Test-takers who find timed, on-screen tasks stressful

What you'll be able to do

  • Understand the current TOEFL iBT format and task types
  • Take useful notes from lectures and conversations
  • Give clear, organised spoken answers within short time limits
  • Write well-structured responses on screen
  • Build the academic vocabulary the test relies on

Study plan

Your study plan

A typical plan for this course. We adjust the pace and focus to your level, your goal and your schedule.

  1. 1

    Weeks 1–2

    Understand the test

    Current format and task types, a diagnostic in each section, and your academic vocabulary baseline.

  2. 2

    Weeks 3–5

    Academic reading and listening

    Reading academic texts efficiently, and note-taking from lectures, conversations and announcements.

  3. 3

    Weeks 6–8

    Speaking and writing

    Timed spoken responses and written tasks with feedback, plus building comfortable typing speed.

  4. 4

    Weeks 9–12

    Get test-ready

    Full practice tests, a review of weak areas, and a clear plan for test day.

How you learn

In person or online, your choice

The same course, the same study plan and the same personal feedback, whichever format suits you.

In person

Face-to-face sessions for learners who like to practise in the room, with real-time conversation and feedback.

Online, over a video call

Live sessions from wherever you are. All you need is a stable internet connection and a device with a microphone.

What's included

  • Live, interactive sessions
  • Feedback on spoken and written responses
  • Academic vocabulary and note-taking practice
  • Timed practice tests
