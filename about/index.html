About Humans of English

English is more than a subject. It's a way to connect.

We help learners communicate clearly, prepare for the opportunities that matter to them, and develop real confidence through practical English education.

Our mission

Every learner has a story. English helps them tell it.

Behind every exam score is a person with a plan: a university place, a new job, a move abroad, or simply the wish to be understood.

Our name says what we care about most. English is learned by humans, with humans, for human reasons. Grammar, vocabulary and exam technique all matter, but they matter because they help people connect, take part and be heard.

That's why we combine structured preparation for exams like IELTS and TOEFL with the everyday communication skills that make English feel like your own: speaking naturally, listening with confidence and expressing ideas clearly.

What we believe

The principles behind our teaching

Practical first

Every lesson connects to a real situation, whether that's a conversation, an email, an interview or an exam task. If you can't use it, we haven't finished teaching it.

Respect for every learner

Learners come from every background, age and level. Your first language and experience are strengths to build on, not problems to fix.

Clear, honest goals

We help you set specific goals and show you how to measure progress towards them. We won't promise scores. We'll help you work towards yours.

Confidence through practice

Confidence isn't a personality trait. It grows from preparation, repetition and supportive feedback, and we build all three into every pathway.

Our commitments

What you can expect from us

Trust matters in education. These are the promises we make to every learner.

  • Honesty about results. We won't promise exam scores or show invented success stories. Your progress depends on your starting point and practice, and we'll be open about that.
  • Clear information. We'll explain what a course or pathway includes before you commit to anything.
  • Useful feedback. Specific, kind and actionable, so you always know what to work on next.
  • Free resources for everyone. Our learning guides are open to all learners, whether or not you study with us.
  • Respect for your privacy. We only ask for the information we need. Read our privacy policy.
